What the data tells us

Three evidence-backed observations and business implications

↗ Seasonal revenue peak

November 2011 reached £1.50M, about 30.6% higher than October. Plan capacity ahead of year-end demand, but don't infer a multi-year seasonal forecast.

◎ UK market concentration

The United Kingdom contributed 84.6% of observed gross positive sales. Explore other markets after evaluating delivery costs and profitability.

↺ Repeat purchasing

2,845 of 4,338 identifiable customers had more than one distinct invoice (65.6%). Customers with missing IDs cannot be classified.

Dataset, validation & assumptions

Source: UCI Online Retail, Dec 2010–Dec 9, 2011. Rebuilt from the original 541,909-row workbook; excluded 5,268 exact duplicates, 2,512 invalid lines and 9,251 return/cancellation lines, retaining 524,878 positive sales lines. The CSV pipeline was independently reconciled against the full-workbook benchmarks, and SQL KPI queries were verified. These figures represent positive-line gross sales, not net realized revenue; returns are omitted rather than netted, and individual tax and shipping details are not modeled. All filters on this dashboard operate only on the labeled chart/card scope; aggregate tables are not joined at transaction level. No customer-level data is embedded in this page.
